# Mathematical handoff — 2026-08-17

## Status

The problem is **not solved**. No valid 3×3 semimagic square of nine distinct positive cubes is known from this project, and no general impossibility proof has been obtained.

## General exact reduction

Let the top-row bases be `(a,b,c)` and let

```text
S = a^3 + b^3 + c^3.
```

Column sums equal `S` exactly when

```text
d^3 + g^3 = b^3 + c^3
e^3 + h^3 = a^3 + c^3
f^3 + i^3 = a^3 + b^3.
```

Define

```text
X = d^3 - g^3
Y = e^3 - h^3
Z = f^3 - i^3.
```

Then both lower rows have sum `S` exactly iff

```text
X + Y + Z = 0.
```

This is necessary and sufficient for the general problem.

## Exact finite search already completed

For bases up to 50,000 the full reduction above gave:

```text
1,250,025,000 pairs
359,210 sums with multiple representations
720,173 active edges
725,448 alternatives
38,067 possible top-row triangles
39,658 alternative triples
0 exact cancellations
```

The sign of the cancellation defect changes across this corpus, so a naive universal inequality in one direction is false.

## Special searches already completed

- Morgenstern equal-difference construction through 50,000: all 1,628,027 ratio matches were scale copies; no non-scale candidate.
- Corrected Morgenstern equal-sum/four-cycle construction through 50,000: no usable simple four-cycle.
- Euler parametric four-cycle search in `1<=r<=400`, `-r<=q<=r`, `-2r<=p<=2r`: 7,132,254 positive identities and no simple four-cycle.
- General Euler-ratio triangle search at parameter denominator limit 60: 24,080 positive identities, 48,148 distinct ratios, 592 multiplicative ratio triangles, 626 alternative triples, zero exact cancellations.

These are finite negative results, not impossibility proofs.

## Infinite classes already excluded by proof

Do not reopen without finding an error:

1. additive bases `z_ij = u_i + v_j`;
2. affine-rank-one bases `z_ij = t + x_i y_j`;
3. nine bases semimagic simultaneously in powers 1 and 3;
4. both homogeneous orientations of the special one-parameter Euler family;
5. the integer Euler subfamily with ratio `4/(3n^2-6n+8)` for a three-edge homogeneous triangle, by a mod-3 obstruction.

## Best current frontier

For the special one-parameter Euler identity

```text
P=1-u+3u^2
Q=1+u+3u^2
C=1-2u-3u^3
D=1+2u+3u^3
P^3+Q^3=C^3+D^3,
```

the two homogeneous three-edge orientations are excluded. The surviving mixed cases reduce, after exact elimination of the third parameter, to four sign-changing irreducible factors over Q with bidegrees

```text
(26,22), (27,27), (27,27), (26,24).
```

No positive rational point is known and no local/global obstruction has been proved for all four. This is the primary target of Run 000.

## Important dead ends

- Do not spend the first 80-core run simply repeating the complete base search with a slightly larger bound.
- Do not infer impossibility from the Morgenstern or Euler construction searches.
- Do not treat a very small numerical defect as a solution.
- Do not use floating point to decide equality or rationality.
- Dense cube-sum graphs from the literature do not automatically contain the precise structure needed by the semimagic reduction.

## Candidate acceptance

A candidate counts only after exact reconstruction to nine positive pairwise-distinct integer bases and exact equality of all six cube sums, followed by an independent verifier.
