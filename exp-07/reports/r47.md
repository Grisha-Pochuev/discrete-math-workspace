# r47 Markoff-root reconstruction and partial Vieta descent

This note makes the local correspondence of `r45` completely explicit.

## 1. Linear reconstruction of the four roots

Let positive integers `C,P,Q,k` satisfy the Markoff-type equation

```text
2CPQ=3k(C^2+P^2+Q^2+3k^2).                      (1)
```

Define

```text
x = (C-P-Q-3k)/2,
y = (C-P+Q+3k)/2,
z = (C+P-Q+3k)/2,
w = (C+P+Q-3k)/2.                               (2)
```

Then direct inversion gives

```text
P=(z-x)-3k,
Q=(y-x)-3k,
C=x+w+3k.                                        (3)
```

The four roots in (2) are integers precisely when the numerators have the required parity.  The strict positive cube-rectangle branch is characterized by

```text
P>3k,
Q>3k,
C>P+Q+3k.                                        (4)
```

Indeed, (4) gives `x>0`, while the other three roots in (2) are then automatically positive.  Moreover

```text
z-x=P+3k>6k,
y-x=Q+3k>6k,
y+z-x-w=6k.                                     (5)
```

Substituting (2) into the cube equality shows that (1) is exactly equivalent to

```text
x^3+w^3=y^3+z^3.                                 (6)
```

Distinctness inside the rectangle additionally requires `P!=Q` (so `y!=z`); the other strict inequalities already separate the remaining pairs.

## 2. The physical branch is the larger Vieta root

Viewing (1) as a quadratic in `C`, its second root is

```text
C'=(P^2+Q^2+3k^2)/C
  =2PQ/(3k)-C.                                   (7)
```

The square-deficit invariant is

```text
m=3kC-PQ>0.                                      (8)
```

For the second root,

```text
m'=3kC'-PQ=-m.                                   (9)
```

Thus the positive-root cube rectangle always chooses the larger Vieta branch.

## 3. Strict height decrease

Assume without loss of generality

```text
P>=Q.
```

From the physical inequality `C>P+Q+3k`,

```text
CP > P(P+Q+3k)
   > P^2+Q^2+3k^2,                               (10)
```

because

```text
P(P+Q+3k)-(P^2+Q^2+3k^2)
 = Q(P-Q)+3k(P-k)>0.
```

Combining (7) and (10) gives

```text
0<C'<P=max(P,Q).                                 (11)
```

So the Vieta move in the physical largest coordinate strictly lowers the height over the rationals.

## 4. An integral descent criterion

Reducing (1) modulo `C` gives

```text
C | 3k(P^2+Q^2+3k^2).                            (12)
```

Therefore, whenever

```text
gcd(C,3k)=1,                                     (13)
```

we have

```text
C | P^2+Q^2+3k^2,
```

and hence the smaller root `C'` in (7) is a positive integer.

Thus every physical local block satisfying (13) admits a **strict integer Vieta descent** to another positive integer point of the same Markoff surface.  The descended point is outside the physical `m>0` branch in the `C` direction by (9), but the symmetric equation allows further permutations and Vieta moves.

The obstruction to turning this immediately into a complete local classification is now precise: an integer descent can stop only when the current largest coordinate shares a prime factor with `3k`.  This ties the Vieta mechanism directly to the prime-support restrictions in `r34-r46`.
