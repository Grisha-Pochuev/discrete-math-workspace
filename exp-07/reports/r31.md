# r31 modular Vandermonde filter

This note gives a universal congruence condition for the Cartesian additive cube grid from `r20`.  It is independent of the finite `D` frontier and of the `r27-r30` gap reductions.

## 1. Coordinate form

Write the nine cube values as

```text
Ai + Bj,   i,j in {0,1,2},
```

with three integer row coordinates `A0,A1,A2` and three integer column coordinates `B0,B1,B2`.  Every one of the nine sums is an integer cube.

For a modulus `m`, let `Cm` be the set of cubic residues modulo `m`.

For

```text
m in {4,7,9,13}
```

the residue sets are exactly

```text
C4  = {0,1,3},
C7  = {0,1,6},
C9  = {0,1,8},
C13 = {0,1,5,8,12}.
```

A direct finite check gives, for every nonzero residue `t` modulo any one of these four moduli,

```text
|Cm intersect (Cm-t)| <= 2.                       (1)
```

## 2. At least one repeated coordinate residue in each direction pair

Suppose the three `B` coordinates occupy at least two distinct residue classes modulo `m`.  Pick two of them whose difference is nonzero, say `t`.

For every row coordinate `Ai`, both

```text
Ai+Bj,
Ai+Bk
```

are cube residues.  After translating by `Bj`, the residue of `Ai` must therefore lie in

```text
Cm intersect (Cm-t).
```

By (1), the three `A` coordinates can occupy at most two residue classes.

The same argument with `A` and `B` interchanged shows:

- if one coordinate triple occupies at least two residue classes, the other occupies at most two;
- if one coordinate triple occupies only one class, then all three of its pairwise differences are divisible by `m`.

Consequently, in every case the product of the two 3-point Vandermonde factors is divisible by `m^2`:

```text
m^2 |
 (A1-A0)(A2-A0)(A2-A1)
 (B1-B0)(B2-B0)(B2-B1).                           (2)
```

If both coordinate triples use two classes, each direction contributes at least one factor of `m`.  If one triple uses only one class, that direction alone contributes at least `m^3`, so (2) still follows.

## 3. Combined universal divisor

The four moduli

```text
4,7,9,13
```

are pairwise coprime.  Multiplying the four consequences (2) gives

```text
(4*7*9*13)^2 = 10,732,176
```

as a universal divisor:

```text
10,732,176 |
 (A1-A0)(A2-A0)(A2-A1)
 (B1-B0)(B2-B0)(B2-B1).                           (3)
```

In the positive normalization of `r20`, the column differences are

```text
D, E, E-D,
```

and the row differences are

```text
K01, K02, K12.
```

Thus every candidate necessarily satisfies

```text
10,732,176 | D*E*(E-D)*K01*K02*K12.               (4)
```

By `r20/r22`, each of these six positive coordinate differences is itself represented as a difference of positive cubes in at least three ways.  Equation (4) is therefore a cheap exact modular filter on the six repeated-difference coordinates, not merely on individual cell residues.
