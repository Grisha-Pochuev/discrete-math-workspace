# r43 nonlinear side-product determinant

This note combines the exact local norm identity of `r39` with the root-gap total positivity of `r36`.

## 1. Four local side products

For the four adjacent root rectangles, define the product of their four positive root-edge lengths:

```text
N0 = a(a-d) h(h-d),
N1 = (a-d)(a-d-e) j(j-e),
N2 = b(b-f) (h-d)(h-d-f),
N3 = (b-f)(b-f-g) (j-e)(j-e-g).                 (1)
```

By the local square identity in `r39`, with

```text
d=6k0, e=6k1, f=6k2, g=6k3,
```

and the corresponding positive square-deficit integers `m0,...,m3`,

```text
Ni = mi^2 + 108 ki^4.                             (2)
```

So every `Ni` is simultaneously a product of four root gaps and a quadratic norm-type expression in the two local integer invariants `mi,ki`.

## 2. Determinant factorization

Direct cancellation gives

```text
N0*N3-N1*N2
 = (a-d)(b-f)(h-d)(j-e)
   * [a h (b-f-g)(j-e-g)
      - b j (a-d-e)(h-d-f)].                     (3)
```

Every factor outside the square bracket is positive.

From the two-step form of the `r36` contraction inequalities,

```text
(a-d-e)/a < (b-f-g)/b,
(h-d-f)/h < (j-e-g)/j.                           (4)
```

Equivalently,

```text
a(b-f-g) > b(a-d-e),
h(j-e-g) > j(h-d-f).                             (5)
```

Multiplying the two strict positive inequalities (5) makes the square bracket in (3) strictly positive. Therefore

```text
N0*N3 > N1*N2.                                   (6)
```

Since the `Ni` are positive integers,

```text
N0*N3-N1*N2 >= 1.                                (7)
```

## 3. Pure `k,m` form

Using (2), every candidate must satisfy the nonlinear integer inequality

```text
(m0^2+108k0^4)(m3^2+108k3^4)
 >
(m1^2+108k1^4)(m2^2+108k2^4).                   (8)
```

This must hold simultaneously with

```text
k0*k3 > k1*k2,                                   (r28)
m0*m3 > m1*m2,                                   (r39)
```

and the four quotient-order inequalities of `r40`.

Thus the same four adjacent rectangles carry at least three different strictly positive determinant constraints: one linear in the cube-root mixed deficits `k`, one linear in the square-root mixed deficits `m`, and the nonlinear norm combination (8).
