# r49 explicit quotient tower over Q

This note makes the seven positive-genus quotient curves behind `r48` completely explicit over the rational field.

## 1. Function-field form

On the affine chart `T=1`, the fixed-layer closure curve is

```text
Q^3=P^3+A,
R^3=P^3+B,
```

where

```text
A=b^3-a^3,
B=c^3-a^3,
0<A<B.
```

Put

```text
z=P^3.
```

Then

```text
P^3=z,
Q^3=z+A,
R^3=z+B.                                         (1)
```

For exponents `i,j,k in {0,1,2}`, the monomial

```text
Y=P^i Q^j R^k
```

satisfies the cyclic cubic equation

```text
Y^3=z^i(z+A)^j(z+B)^k.                           (2)
```

Multiplying `(i,j,k)` by `2 mod 3` gives the same character line, so only one representative of each pair is needed.

## 2. The four elliptic quotients

The four character patterns with quotient genus one are

```text
(1,1,0):  Y^3=z(z+A),
(1,0,1):  Y^3=z(z+B),
(0,1,1):  Y^3=(z+A)(z+B),
(1,1,1):  Y^3=z(z+A)(z+B).                       (3)
```

The quotient maps are simply

```text
Y=PQ,
Y=PR,
Y=QR,
Y=PQR,
```

respectively.

For the first three curves, two finite zeros of the right side are branched and infinity is the third branch point.  For the last curve all three finite zeros are branched while infinity is unbranched.  A degree-3 cyclic cover of `P^1` fully ramified at three points has genus one.

These are the four rational elliptic quotients corresponding to the four elliptic factors in the type `(3,3)` decomposition of Carvacho--Hidalgo--Quispe.

The first three are equivalent to the three difference cubics already displayed in `r48`.  The fourth is equivalent to the diagonal cubic obtained by eliminating the common shift:

```text
B Q^3-A R^3+(A-B)P^3=0.                          (4)
```

## 3. The three genus-2 quotients

The remaining positive-genus character patterns are

```text
(2,1,1):  Y^3=z^2(z+A)(z+B),
(1,2,1):  Y^3=z(z+A)^2(z+B),
(1,1,2):  Y^3=z(z+A)(z+B)^2.                     (5)
```

with quotient maps

```text
Y=P^2 Q R,
Y=P Q^2 R,
Y=P Q R^2.                                       (6)
```

For each curve in (5), all three finite points

```text
0,-A,-B
```

are branched and the exponent sum is nonzero modulo 3, so infinity is also branched.  Thus there are four fully ramified points.  Riemann--Hurwitz gives

```text
2g-2=3*(-2)+4*(3-1)=2,
```

hence

```text
g=2.                                              (7)
```

All three genus-2 curves and all three maps (6) are therefore defined over `Q`; no choice of `sqrt(lambda)` or cube roots of `A` is needed.

## 4. Dimension check

The total quotient genus is

```text
4*1+3*2=10,                                      (8)
```

exactly the genus of the full closure curve from `r48`.

The general generalized-Fermat decomposition theorem identifies these seven quotients as an isogeny decomposition of the genus-10 Jacobian into

```text
4 elliptic curves + 3 genus-2 Jacobians.          (9)
```

Over the complex numbers, each genus-2 Jacobian in (9) further splits into two elliptic curves, giving ten elliptic dimensions in total.  The equations (5) show precisely which three rational abelian surfaces must be studied to determine whether that final `2+2+2` splitting descends to `Q` or occurs only after an extension.

## 5. Arithmetic significance

A rational layer point `(P,Q,R)` simultaneously determines rational points on all seven curves (3) and (5).  Conversely, arbitrary points on the seven factors do not automatically glue: their `z` coordinates and monomial values must come from one common triple `(P,Q,R)`.

Thus the fixed-layer closure problem can now be attacked as a compatibility problem among four elliptic curves and three explicit genus-2 curves, rather than as a black-box genus-10 rational-point search.
