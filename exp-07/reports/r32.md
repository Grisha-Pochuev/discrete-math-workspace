# r32 prime-power sharpening

This note sharpens the universal modular divisor from `r31` by using prime-power information rather than only the moduli `4` and `9`.

Let the Cartesian additive cube grid be

```text
Ai+Bj,   i,j in {0,1,2},
```

and define the two 3-point Vandermonde products

```text
VA=(A1-A0)(A2-A0)(A2-A1),
VB=(B1-B0)(B2-B0)(B2-B1).
```

Every `Ai+Bj` is an integer cube.

## 1. Exact finite residue minima

For a prime power `p^k`, residues modulo `p^k` give a rigorous lower bound for the `p`-adic valuation of each coordinate difference.  If two residues coincide modulo `p^k`, the corresponding difference has valuation at least `k`; otherwise its valuation is the valuation of their nonzero residue difference.

Translation of the additive decomposition lets us normalize

```text
A0=0
```

without changing any sums or coordinate differences.

The finite exact certificate `src/r32.py` enumerates every normalized residue configuration compatible with all nine sums being cube residues and obtains the exact minima

```text
mod 8:   v2(VA*VB) >= 6,
mod 27:  v3(VA*VB) >= 6,
mod 7:   v7(VA*VB) >= 2,
mod 13:  v13(VA*VB) >= 2.                         (1)
```

The first two bounds strictly improve the `4^2` and `9^2` contributions recorded in `r31`.

## 2. Sharpened universal divisor

The four prime-power conditions in (1) are independent, hence

```text
2^6 * 3^6 * 7^2 * 13^2 = 386,358,336
```

divides the product:

```text
386,358,336 | VA*VB.                              (2)
```

In the positive normalization of `r20`,

```text
VB = D*E*(E-D)
```

up to sign, while

```text
VA = K01*K02*K12
```

up to sign.  Therefore every candidate satisfies

```text
386,358,336 |
D*E*(E-D)*K01*K02*K12.                            (3)
```

All six factors in (3) are positive repeated differences of cubes with at least three representations by `r20/r22`.

Compared with `r31`, the guaranteed divisor has increased by the factor

```text
36.
```

The result is universal: it uses only the additive-grid structure and exact cube residues, with no bound on the size of the roots or offsets.

Exact finite certificate: `exp-07/src/r32.py`.
