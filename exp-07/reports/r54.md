# r54 — rational activation and bounded primitive-ALL3 audit

This note records two exact consequences of the overflow-free closure viewpoint of r52, plus an independent bounded search intended to falsify the empirical "primitive ALL3" hypothesis.

## 1. Scaling only clears rational denominators

Fix a two-layer strip

```text
A^3-a^3 = B^3-b^3 = C^3-c^3 = D,
a<b<c.
```

Put

```text
K01=b^3-a^3,
K12=c^3-b^3,
K02=c^3-a^3.
```

If a transverse difference `K` has a positive rational representation

```text
y^3-x^3=K,
```

with `x=u/g, y=v/g`, then

```text
v^3-u^3 = g^3 K.
```

Conversely every integer representation of `g^3 K`, after division by `g`, is a rational representation of `K` whose reduced denominator divides `g`.

Therefore multiplying a six-root strip by `g` cannot create new rational geometry. It merely makes rational transverse representations with denominators dividing `g` integral.

For the primitive strip

```text
(51,72,115) -> (162,165,178),   D=4118877,
```

the two transverse differences with only two integral representations are

```text
K01=240597,
K02=1388224.
```

At scale `g=63`, the extra rational positive representations are

```text
K01: (666/7, 723/7),
K02: (32/9, 1004/9),
```

while `K12=1147627` already has the integral third representation `(618,619)`. Thus `lcm(7,1,9)=63`, explaining exactly why the scale 63 passes the integer ALL3 filter.

At scale `g=469=7*67`, `K01` again uses denominator 7 and `K02` has the extra representation

```text
(17648/67, 18084/67),
```

so `lcm(7,67)=469`. This independently explains the other ALL3 scaling of the same primitive core.

## 2. Exact closure is scale-invariant over Q

The r52 common-lower-endpoint criterion extends verbatim over the rationals. A genuine third layer exists iff there is a positive rational `p` for which

```text
q^3-p^3=K01,
r^3-p^3=K02
```

for rational `q,r`, with `p` distinct from the two known lower endpoints.

If `p=u/v` in lowest terms, then the two equations necessarily take the integral homogeneous form

```text
s^3-u^3 = K01*v^3,
t^3-u^3 = K02*v^3.
```

Hence an integer solution at any scale is exactly a rational point on the primitive fixed-base closure curve. Integer ALL3 multiplicity is only a denominator-clearing prefilter; it is not the intrinsic closure condition.

## 3. Reznick triple identity cannot give three positive differences

Reznick's three-representation quadratic-form identity (his Eq. 1.3) uses the three pairs

```text
A = 4x^2-4xy+6y^2,
B = 5x^2-5xy-3y^2,
C = 6x^2-4xy+4y^2,
D = 3x^2+5xy-5y^2,
E = 6x^2-8xy+6y^2,
F = 3x^2-11xy+3y^2,
```

with

```text
A^3+B^3 = C^3-D^3 = E^3-F^3.
```

To turn all three into positive differences one would need simultaneously

```text
B<0,
D>0,
F>0.
```

For `t=x/y` these conditions require respectively

```text
-0.421954... < t < 1.421954...,
t > 0.703257...  (within the first interval),
t < 0.296742... or t > 3.369924... .
```

Their intersection is empty. Thus this standard parametric source of three equal sums/differences cannot produce three positive representations in the orientation needed here.

## 4. Independent bounded exact audit

An independent C++ enumeration formed every positive difference

```text
y^3-x^3,  1 <= x < y <= 15000.
```

It processed

```text
112,492,500 pairs,
4,141 values with at least three bounded representations,
4,441 triples of representations.
```

For every one of the 4,441 triples, all positive representations of each of its three transverse differences were then reconstructed exactly by factorization, with no bound on the transverse roots.

Exactly one triple satisfied the ALL3 condition:

```text
D=1029912837219
lower=(3213,4536,7245)
upper=(10206,10395,11214)
gcd(all six roots)=63.
```

This is the already known scaled copy of the primitive `D=4118877` core. There were **zero primitive ALL3 triples** in this audit.

The smaller `N=10000` audit contained 49,995,000 pairs, 2,485 triple-representation values and 2,653 triples; exact transverse reconstruction gave zero ALL3 triples of any kind.

This is finite evidence only. It does not prove the conjecture that every positive integer ALL3 strip is imprimitive.

## 5. Current target

The computational evidence now suggests separating two statements:

1. **integer prefilter conjecture:** every integer ALL3 strip is imprimitive;
2. **actual closure problem:** the two transverse Fermat cubics have no third synchronized positive rational lower endpoint.

Even a proof of (1) would not by itself settle the full problem, because scaling clears denominators. The scale-invariant rational synchronization in (2) is the mathematically decisive target.
