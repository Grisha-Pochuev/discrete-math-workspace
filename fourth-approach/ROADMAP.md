# Fourth approach roadmap

The roadmap is sequential, but the hourly task may advance one safe transition at a time after the preceding evidence is accepted. A finished compute job alone is not permission to advance.

## Run 000 / Stage 0 — source frontier inventory

Freeze immutable commit identifiers and hashes for:

- accepted Third approach 2.0 exact-certificate archives through run `30911346279`;
- old-basin and independent Second approach archives;
- relevant First approach exact obstruction material;
- the ordinary Third approach plateau report.

Output: immutable source manifest, summary, checksums, and human-readable report. This is a four-shard data-processing run, not a 20-job mathematical search.

## Run 001 / Stage 1 — canonicalization and independent verification

Determine how many mathematically distinct exact obstruction classes remain after vertex/color symmetries and duplicate representations are removed. Normalize certificates, group by canonical support and structural signature, and verify exact identities with an independent checker.

Run 001 must not launch until its implementation, specification, tests, and real-path smoke pass. The draft specification may exist before the code is implemented, but validation must reject an unsupported task.

## Stage 2 — certificate minimization

Find the simplest exact mechanism in each canonical class. Test removal of support variables, edge-color types, equations, multiplier monomials, terms, coefficients, degree, and rational complexity. State the exact notion of minimality.

## Stage 3 — one-edit deletion contrasts

For selected minimal supports `S`, test canonical children `S - {e}` and record whether the same obstruction remains, another obstruction appears, a higher degree is needed, the child is unresolved, or it becomes numerically strong.

## Stage 4 — one-edit addition contrasts

For selected unresolved or numerically strong supports `S`, test canonical neighbors `S + {e}`. Prefer causal one-edit pairs controlling all other structure.

## Stage 5 — bridge to the old Second approach basin

Stratify absolute champions, structurally diverse old-basin candidates, support-mutated descendants, and residual quantiles. Record obstruction coverage, minimal certificates, tested degree, nearest contrasts, and unresolved cases.

## Stage 6 — bridge to independent Second approach lineages

Repeat Stage 5 without mixing statistics. Compare class coverage, certificate complexity, survivor rate, structural distance, and residual conditioned on obstruction type.

## Stage 7 — targeted higher-degree tests

Select only a small, canonical, numerically strong set with no low-degree certificate or simple First approach obstruction. Distinguish newly closed cases, incomplete tests, and genuine survivors relative to a precisely stated certificate class.

## Stage 8 — transfer beyond `n=6`

Test hypothesis-driven lifts to `n=8`: embed obstruction cores, add matched vertex pairs, combine overlapping six-vertex identities, and search for parameterized coefficients. One common exact template at `n=6` and `n=8` is more valuable than thousands of new `n=6` certificates.

## Stage 9 — GPT-5.6 Sol cycle

Prepare the compact handoff. Ask Sol to propose structural lemmas, test them against contrast pairs and survivors, repair falsified lemmas using smallest counterexamples, derive consequences for arbitrary even `n`, and request exact computations that distinguish competing generalizations.

Every Sol proposal returns to an exact falsification loop.

## Automation rule

The hourly task may launch the next registered run only after:

- no conflicting large workflow is active or queued;
- the previous archive is accepted and committed;
- the next code path is implemented and smoked;
- its run specification and source commits are fixed;
- no technical or scientific stopping condition applies.

It performs at most one meaningful transition per activation and stops immediately on failure or unexpected data.
